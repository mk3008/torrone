SCREEN-001 / Screen pattern / research example

Work queue with filters above results

The screen keeps search conditions and commands in a stable upper area, with a result area that distinguishes initial, populated and empty outcomes. Reuse the relationship of content areas, not the demo's access-review business vocabulary or entire Application Shell.

Business applicationWeb-originHistorical example · not a new approval

Screen states

Three independent, full-width captures

What this design communicates

Reusable arrangement

  • Group filter inputs, Search and Clear into one recognizable area above results.
  • Use consistent result headings so a returned row is easy to compare.
  • Keep results, not-yet-searched guidance and empty-result feedback within the same region.
  • Explain how to recover from no results while leaving the filters available.

Application-specific choices

  • Which conditions, data columns and commands are required by the Business Design.
  • Backend behavior, auth, latency, pagination, loading/error/retry and actual navigation.
  • Whether a full page, split pane or another composition suits the user's tasks.
  • Platform-standard controls, precise colors and geometry, keyboard/mobile/accessibility testing.
Research fixture boundary: the top-right “REFERENCE SCENARIO” selector exists only to exercise sample states. It is not a production UI requirement. The left navigation is outside this screen pattern; see MENU-001.

Source and limits
