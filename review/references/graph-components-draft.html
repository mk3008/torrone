Scope & handoff notes

Review normal, selected and keyboard-focus states through the controls above. Tab / Shift+Tab move focus; Enter / Space activate. Escape clears the current group's selection. Opening local details focuses the caption; closing returns to the initiating control. Selecting another relation hides the previous detail and any temporarily revealed Read.

Preserve independent kind and scope, selectable outside-scope nodes, relation direction/type/caption, full-caption access, and temporary omitted-Read disclosure with return. Read / Input flows from Object to Business; Write / Output flows from Business to Object. A business Exception returns to a preceding business under its stated condition.

These are draft local choices. Node samples and relation samples are independent review groups, not a linked Viewer. Dimensions, DOM, framework, routing, row placement and this review sheet's layout may vary. No whole-Viewer navigation, graph layout, display modes, pan/zoom or production data is modeled.
