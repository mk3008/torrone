# Experimental selection aid

Read the original entry and its state images before proposing reuse.

- Match the requested responsibility, rather than the nearest shape or sample label. One entry may cover only part of a screen.
- Check that the required states are pictured. A nearby state or another control's state does not fill a missing one.
- Separate appearance reuse from executable behavior and approval. Follow the consuming application's requirements for interaction and business decisions.
- If the index has no matching entry, say so. If material requirements are missing, ask for them and keep any candidates conditional.

This aid restates existing catalog boundaries. It does not add patterns, approval, interaction contracts or business rules.
