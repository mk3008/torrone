Test whether the Manifest's pinned Grid-context contract preserves the minimum
selection and record-identity context while the rest of a horizontally
scrolling result grid moves. The unchanged product prompt and frozen Manifest

captured and evaluated as they are. A failed visual result remains evidence; it
must not be individually regenerated or edited. Changes to the Manifest,
prompt, or fixture require a later numbered attempt with three new runs.
